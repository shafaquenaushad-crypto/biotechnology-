# Role of Genetic Mutations in Human Diseases

This project is about understanding genetic mutations and how changes in DNA can contribute to human diseases.

## Topics Covered
- Introduction to genetic mutations.
- Role of mutations in human diseases.
- Sickle cell disease and the mutation in the HBB gene.
- Cystic fibrosis and the CFTR gene.
- Huntington's disease and CAG repeat expansion in the HTT gene.

## Learning Outcome
Through this project, I learned about different types of genetic mutations and their relationship with human diseases.
